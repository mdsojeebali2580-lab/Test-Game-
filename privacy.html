Privacy Policy

Puzzle Quest processes Telegram user ID, display name and username (if provided), game scores, points, and ad event identifiers to operate accounts, leaderboards, security checks and rewards.

Telegram Mini App authentication data is validated on our server. We do not treat browser-provided Telegram profile fields as trusted until validation succeeds.

Advertising partners may process device and ad interaction data under their own privacy policies when their approved ad formats are shown. We do not control third-party ad targeting or tracking.

We retain game and ad-event records for service operation, fraud prevention and reporting. Contact the app operator through the Telegram bot for privacy requests.

Do not enter passwords, payment card details or sensitive personal information into this game.
